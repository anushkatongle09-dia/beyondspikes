Plot Your Spikes

Explore an illustrative blood-glucose curve over a 12-hour window (2 hours before your first meal through 10 hours after) from USDA food data and meal times. The x-axis shows clock time for that window, anchored to the meal time you pick (for example 6:00 AM). How to add a meal: type a food (2+ letters), wait for matches, click one result in the list (that locks in nutrition), choose a time, then Add meal. Pan and zoom along time only; the glucose axis stays 0–400 mg/dL. Use Reset zoom to fit the full time window again. A built-in offline food list still works if USDA search is busy or unavailable.

    Add a meal to plot a composite curve. Blue dotted lines: 70 and 140 mg/dL references.

    Horizontal dotted lines mark 70 mg/dL and 140 mg/dL. Dashed orange vertical lines mark each meal time; on the curve, each meal is a small colored dot on the line. Meal names sit just under that point with ink-blue background and white text.

    Educational only. This tool is not a medical device and does not predict your real blood sugar. Carbohydrate, fiber, and sugar values come from USDA when available, or from the offline list. Diabetic vs non-diabetic comparison draws two heuristic curves for the same meals (not real CGM file traces). Always follow your clinician’s advice for diabetes care.
