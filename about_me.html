About Me

Anushka Tongle · Rising junior · Canyon Crest Academy (Class of 2028)

anushkatongle09@gmail.com · (858) 649-0214

Hi — I’m Anushka Tongle, a rising junior at Canyon Crest Academy (CCA) in San Diego. I care about how science, health, and everyday choices come together in real life: in the classroom, in competitions, through volunteering, and at home with my family as we learn from continuous glucose monitoring and thoughtful meals.

At school I keep a strong academic focus (4.0 unweighted / 4.5 weighted GPA) while taking challenging coursework in biology, AP Chemistry, and Biotechnology 1. I’m active in Health Occupations Students of America (HOSA) and Science Olympiad, where I’ve enjoyed going deep on subjects like ecology and anatomy & physiology — and coaching younger students at Pacific Trails Middle School.

Science, health, and competitions

I like turning curiosity into results. In Science Olympiad I’ve earned regional and state recognition, including 1st place in Ecology at regionals and state, and 2nd place in Anatomy & Physiology at regionals. Through HOSA I’ve qualified for the California state competition in 2025 and 2026.

Outside of competitions, I’ve built experience in healthcare settings: volunteering in oncology at Scripps La Jolla Memorial Hospital, supporting patients at the VA hospital (including playing piano for people with spinal cord injury), and helping with triage and clinic workflow on a Jamaica medical mission with Nova Southeastern University — where I’ve also contributed to electronic medical record work to improve how patient information is collected.

Music and piano

Piano has been a long-term part of my life — I completed 10 years in MTAC with state honors. I compete through the Southwestern Youth Music Festival, earning 4th place in the Chopin category and 3rd place in the Baroque category. Music isn’t only performance for me: it’s also a way to connect with people, whether that’s playing for patients or supporting activities for older adults living with Alzheimer’s disease.
