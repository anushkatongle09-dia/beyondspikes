Beyond the Spikes is a practical health education project focused on foods, recipes, and daily habits that may help reduce sharp blood sugar spikes in adults and teenagers while supporting steadier energy, better meal choices, and healthier routines at home.[

The site will document real-world food testing using continuous monitoring and related devices on me and my mom, turning personal meal observations into understandable guidance for families, caregivers, and communities that want healthier everyday eating.

The goal of Beyond the Sugar Spikes is not just to talk about “healthy eating” in a general way, but to show how specific meals, ingredients, timing, and portion choices may influence post-meal glucose response and day-long energy.

For teenagers, this matters because puberty and insulin sensitivity can affect how the body responds to carbohydrate quality, and research has linked higher dietary glycemic index during puberty with less favorable later diabetes risk markers.

For older adults, nutrition guidance consistently points toward fruits and vegetables, whole grains, lean proteins, beans, nuts, seeds, hydration, and fewer empty calories as a practical foundation for healthy aging.
